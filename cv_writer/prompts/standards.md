# Standards

These hold for every result, whatever method produced it. A caller cannot
loosen them. Each is written so it can be checked against the finished
output and its inputs.

1. **Grounding.** Every employer, job title, employment date,
   qualification, and metric in the CV appears in, or is directly
   supported by, the candidate's CV or the background documents. Nothing
   factual is introduced that is not in those sources.

2. **No inflation.** Where the sources give no figure for an outcome, the
   CV states no figure for it. Scope, scale, and results are reported at
   the level the sources support and no higher.

3. **Identity block preserved.** The name and contact details at the top
   of the CV are reproduced exactly as they appear at the top of the
   source CV: nothing added, altered, or removed. If the source has no
   such block, the output has none, and the note says so.

4. **Every role kept.** Every employer-and-dates entry in the source work
   history appears in the CV. The detail under a role may be cut; the
   entry itself may not, so no period of employment is hidden.

5. **Gaps recorded, not filled.** A requirement in the job posting that
   the sources do not support is named in the note as a gap. It is not
   answered in the CV by implication or by invention.

6. **No evaluation of the candidate.** Neither the CV nor the note
   praises, criticises, coaches, or judges the candidate's suitability.
   The note records what was done and what the sources do not cover,
   nothing more.

## Precedence

Each layer governs its own domain, and the domains do not overlap, so
most conflicts do not arise. Where a question could fall to more than
one, this is the order.

- **Truth, grounding, and the reply shape.** Standards 1 to 6 and the
  output format decide these. Nothing overrides them: not the method,
  not the style, not a candidate parameter.
- **Document structure and length** — the sections, their order, what
  each entry contains, how long the CV is, and how much detail each
  entry carries. The method decides these. The style has no say in them.
- **Voice, register, spelling, language locale, and word choice.** The
  style decides these. The method has no say in them. An explicit tone
  from the candidate overrides the style for register.
- **CV-format conventions** — the page-count norm, whether to include a
  photo, date of birth, or nationality, and whether the document is
  called a CV or a résumé. An explicit region decides these; without
  one, those of the United Kingdom. This is separate from the style's
  language locale: a CV may be written in one country's English while
  following another country's format conventions.
- **Length target.** An explicit target length decides it; without one,
  the region's norm, applied by the method.
