# House Style

These rules apply to all generated text, across every module that
produces prose.

## Language

Write in British English throughout: spelling (organise, colour, centre,
recognise), vocabulary, and punctuation. Write dates as 4 March 2025. Use
single quotation marks, and double quotation marks only inside them.

## Punctuation

Do not use em dashes. Use a full stop where the two parts stand as
separate sentences, a comma where the second part continues the first, or
parentheses for an aside the sentence does not need.

En dashes are correct for number and date ranges (2020–2024). Hyphens are
correct for compounds (cross-team, well-known).

## Voice

Plain, specific, and even in pace. State what is true rather than what is
missing. Prefer the active voice and name who acts. Let the substance
make the point, with no heroic or breathless language.

## CV specifics

- Each experience bullet starts with a strong past-tense verb.
- Keep figures as the source gives them. Do not round for effect.
- Dates as "March 2021 – present" or "June 2018 – February 2021".
