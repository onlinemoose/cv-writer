# Style

How the prose reads. An operator can replace this whole file with their
own house style; the standards, the output format, and the method still
hold.

## Language

Write in British English: organise, colour, recognise, centre. Dates in
prose as 4 March 2025. Single quotation marks, double only inside them.

## Punctuation

Do not use em dashes. Use a full stop where the two parts stand as
separate sentences, a comma where the second part continues the first, or
parentheses for an aside the sentence does not need.

En dashes are correct for number and date ranges (2020–2024). Hyphens are
correct for compounds (cross-team, well-known).

## Voice

Plain, specific, and even in pace. State what is true rather than what is
missing. Prefer the active voice and name who acts. Let the substance
make the point, with no heroic or breathless language.

## Words to avoid

These read as filler or marketing copy, and a precise word almost always
exists. Avoid: leverage, utilise, spearhead, passionate, driven,
results-oriented, dynamic, synergy, thought leader, game-changer,
best-in-class, seamless, cutting-edge, deep dive, circle back.
